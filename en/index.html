Claim flow

At which stage do I work?

A JKN claim passes through seven stages. I spend most of my time on the yellow one.

  1. 1

    Care and medical record

    Doctors and nurses record the condition, the examinations and the procedures.

    Daily Inpatient Screening, while the patient is admitted
  2. 2

    Coding

    Diagnoses and procedures receive ICD-10 and ICD-9-CM codes.

  3. 3

    Grouping

    The codes are grouped into a tariff group under INA-CBGs or iDRG.

  4. 4
    Where I work most

    Internal verification

    Before the claim is submitted, I check that the diagnosis, procedures, examinations and medical record agree and are consistent.

  5. 5

    Submission and BPJS verification

    The claim is submitted to BPJS Kesehatan and verified.

  6. 6

    Pending or dispute

    A claim that is held or contested is answered with the medical record and regulations.

    Jawab Pending
  7. 7

    Post-claim review

    After payment, a claim can be reviewed again through VPK and AAK.

Pending, disputes and VPK/AAK are part of my work too. But stage 4 is where I can stop problems before they start.

Glossary

Claim terms, no jargon.

KCMG group
4case type
10sequence number
Iseverity level

Format of an INA-CBGs code, shown as an example. Not a real case.

Casemix

Grouping patient cases that are similar in diagnosis, procedures and resources used, as a basis for setting tariffs.

INA-CBGs

The package payment system in JKN. It sets the hospital's claim amount from the diagnosis and procedures, with three severity levels: mild, moderate and severe.

iDRG

A diagnosis-based grouping and payment system designed to move JKN on from INA-CBGs. It extends severity to five levels: mild, mild-moderate, moderate, moderate-severe and severe.

VPK (post-claim verification)

A review by BPJS Kesehatan of claims that have already been paid, checking they were correct.

AAK (claim administration audit)

A deeper audit of claims that have been paid, triggered by certain risk signals, including a repeated VPK finding.

Pending and dispute

A pending claim is held until the hospital completes or explains it. A dispute is when the hospital contests a verification result and argues its case.
